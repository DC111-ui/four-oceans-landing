What We Do

Eight ways we move your business forward

One partner across procurement, logistics, storage and relocations, so you're not juggling four suppliers to get one job done.

Procurement

Building Materials

Flooring, bathroom fittings, electrical, paint and waterproofing, sourced in South Africa and delivered to Zimbabwe. One supplier, one shipment, lower logistics cost.

Groceries

Authentic South African products, bulk orders and door-to-door delivery. Everything SA has to offer, delivered to Zimbabwe.

Automotive Parts

Genuine vehicle spares and service kits, sourced in South Africa and delivered to Zimbabwe. Guaranteed fit, manufacturer warranty, longer lifespan.

Appliances

Premium and local brands, sourced in South Africa and delivered to Zimbabwe, from everyday essentials to luxury appliances.

Sourced from South Africa's trusted retailers and brands, including CTM, Italtile, Builders Warehouse, Makro, Woolworths, Dis-Chem, LG, Samsung, Bosch and more, then delivered door to door in Zimbabwe.

Logistics, Storage & Moving

Cross-Border Logistics

Freight, customs documentation and border compliance from Pretoria to Beitbridge. Fleet from bakkies to 30-ton trucks.

Furniture Relocations

Household and apartment moves, packed and handled by an experienced team. Guide price: R1,500–R3,000+.

Student Storage

Affordable, secure storage for University of Pretoria (UP) students in Hatfield, over holidays, internships and lease transitions.

Student Relocations

Hatfield move-outs for UP students, done right and on short notice. Available year-round.
